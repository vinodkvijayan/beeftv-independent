# AI character replacement studio plug-in (`beeftv-plugin-character-replacement`)

`beeftv-plugin-character-replacement` is an AI character replacement plug-in for short dramas and film and television post-production. It adopts the proven **"key frame accurate replacement + video element replacement (`replace_element`)" dual-stage high-fidelity pipeline**, and supports the lossless insertion of the replaced shots into the full film timeline:

- **Two-stage high-fidelity pipeline**:
  1. **Step 2 Keyframe character replacement**: Based on the original shot keyframe (Figure 1) and the target character reference picture (Figure 2), compile a structured prompt word containing precise orientation (single person/left/middle/right), 5-speed replacement range (complete character/currently visible part/face and hair/clothing/arms and hands only) and occlusion protection constraints, or generate a multi-person positioning frame SVG The auxiliary picture produces substitution key frames whose composition, light and shadow, and hand-held props are exactly the same as those of other characters.
  2. **Step 3 Video Action and Character Replacement**: Connect the "original shot video + Step 2 key frames of the good actor" to the downstream video node at the same time, and automatically configure `videoEditOperation = "replace_element"` (the frame and duration automatically follow the original video), and complete the video-level character replacement on the premise of retaining 100% of the original film's actions, props and rival actors.
- **Step 4 Lossless interpolation of the entire film timeline**:
  - Supports pressing `[startMs, endMs]` to cut the replaced shot into the original full-film video, automatically constructing the Timeline v2 of "original film front part → replacement shot → original film back part", and can retain the original film's continuous and synchronized sound track with one click.
- **Multiple portal integration**:
  - **Canvas Node (`character-replacement`)**: Configure camera character mapping directly in the canvas and generate downstream "keyframe substitution + video character substitution" nodes and connections with one click.
  - **Replacement Studio (`/p/beeftv-plugin-character-replacement` with Canvas Sidebar)**: Four-step full-process workbench.
  - **Creation Assistant Tools (`character_replacement_arrange` / `character_replacement_splice_timeline`) and Skills (`character-replacement`)**: Support the assistant to automatically complete two-stage substitution arrangement and full-film insertion through natural language dialogue.
